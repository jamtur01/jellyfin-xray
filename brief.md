Title: Build a Jellyfin “X-Ray” style plugin that shows cast and role metadata during playback

Goal: Replicate Prime Video’s “X-Ray” experience inside Jellyfin. While a video is playing, a user can click an “X-Ray” button to view a panel overlay showing:

Title, season and episode context

Cast list with character names and headshots
